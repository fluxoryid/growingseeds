Install GrowingSeeds

Apple only allows installing from Safari — 3 quick taps and it's on your home screen, full-screen, no address bar.

This install guide is for iPhone/iPad Safari. On Android? Tap here instead.
1
⬆️
Tap the Share icon in Safari's toolbar (the square with an arrow, usually at the bottom of the screen)
2
📜
Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen"
3
✅
Tap "Add" in the top right — the GrowingSeeds icon appears on your home screen
